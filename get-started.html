Get started with Gener-8

Your device

The Gener-8 app is available for iPhone.

Patients: scan the label on your Gener-8 with your iPhone camera to get started.

I’m a patient

  1. Download the Gener-8 app.
  2. Sign up and choose “Patient”.
  3. Tap Connect device and scan the label on your Gener-8, or type the code printed on itthe code .

I’m a therapist

  1. Download the Gener-8 app.
  2. Sign up and choose “Therapist”.
  3. Your patients add you from Care Team in their app.
